## 1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how Mathematador uses cookies and similar technologies (such as on-device local storage) across the web and mobile versions of the Service, and what choices you have. It should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](/info/gdpr).

Unlike many websites, the Mathematador app does not set traditional browser cookies of its own today. Instead, it uses **local device storage** - `AsyncStorage` in the mobile app, and the browser's local storage on the web version - to remember things like your session and saved progress. We're covering that here anyway, in the interest of transparency, because it serves the same practical purpose as a cookie and raises the same kinds of questions (what is stored, why, and how you can clear it).

## 2. Categories we use

### Strictly necessary (always active)

Used to keep the core Service working. We cannot offer signed-in gameplay without these.

| What | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Session / login token | Keeps you signed in between visits, so you don't have to log in every time |
| Local game-progress cache | Lets the game work smoothly and show your progress instantly, ahead of syncing with our servers |
| Consent record | Remembers that you've already responded to our in-app consent notice, so we don't ask again every session |

### Functional

Not currently in use beyond what's listed as strictly necessary above. If we add functional preferences (for example, remembering a display or accessibility setting), they will be listed here.

### Analytics

**Not currently used.** The Service does not run any third-party analytics tool (such as Google Analytics) today. If we introduce analytics in the future, this section will be updated with the specific tool, the data it collects, and how to opt out, before it goes live.

### Advertising

**Not currently used.** The Service does not display advertising today. When advertising is introduced, this section will list the specific ad network(s) and any cookies or identifiers they use, consistent with our commitment (see our [Privacy Policy](/info/gdpr)) to use only advertising appropriate for a Service that may be used by children - contextual rather than behavioral targeting wherever the user is, or may be, a child.

## 3. Third-party cookies

If you sign in with Google, Google's own sign-in service (Google Identity Services) may set cookies in your browser as part of that process. Those cookies are set and controlled by Google, not by us, and are governed by [Google's own privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and cookie information, not this page.

## 4. Consent

Before you can play, we ask you to accept a combined notice covering data processing under GDPR and (in anticipation of future advertising) ad personalization - see our [Privacy Policy](/info/gdpr) for the details of what that consent covers today and what it will cover once advertising launches. We record your acceptance as described in Section 2. There is currently no separate, granular toggle to accept some categories (e.g. strictly necessary) but not others, because only strictly-necessary storage is in use today; once we introduce categories that require separate consent (such as advertising), we will add the corresponding controls.

## 5. Managing local storage and cookies

You can clear locally stored app data at any time:

- **Mobile app**: through your device's app settings (e.g. "Clear storage" / "Clear data" for the app on Android, or by uninstalling and reinstalling on iOS).
- **Web version**: through your browser's site-data or privacy settings for mathematador.net.

Clearing local storage will sign you out and remove any locally cached data, but any progress already saved to your account on our servers is not lost - it will sync back down the next time you sign in.

We may update this policy as the Service changes - in particular when we introduce analytics or advertising. We will post the updated policy here with a new "last updated" date.
