## 1. Why this page exists

Artificial intelligence played a real part in building Mathematador, and we think that's worth being upfront about - both how we used it, and, just as importantly, where we currently don't use it inside the game itself. This page is our transparency disclosure for both.

## 2. How AI was used to build Mathematador

Large parts of this codebase - including game features, backend services, and the content pages you're reading right now - were written with the assistance of AI coding tools (such as Anthropic's Claude Code), directed and reviewed by human developers at every step. AI assistance here means things like drafting code changes, refactoring, writing tests, and preparing documentation from human instructions and review - it does not mean the software runs itself without human oversight. Every change is reviewed before it ships.

## 3. AI inside the game today

As of this writing, Mathematador does **not** use artificial intelligence or machine learning to generate content or make decisions about you while you play:

- **Math exercises** are generated using deterministic, hand-written algorithms (procedural generation based on your level and chosen operation) - not a machine-learning model, and not personalized using an AI system.
- **The Toro Numérico's** hints, encouragement, and reactions are pre-written by our team, not generated live by an AI system.
- There is no chatbot, AI assistant, or generative-AI feature anywhere in the player-facing game.
- We do not use your personal data or gameplay data to train any AI or machine-learning model.

## 4. If that changes

If we introduce an AI-driven feature in the game itself in the future - for example, AI-adjusted difficulty, personalized hints, or generated practice content - we will:

- update this page in advance of launch, describing what the feature does and what data it uses;
- clearly tell you when you are interacting with an AI system, consistent with the transparency obligations of the EU AI Act (Article 50) and equivalent rules elsewhere;
- label any AI-generated media (such as AI-generated images, audio, or video) as such, in a way that's detectable, if we publish any; and
- seek your consent first, where the feature would use your personal data in a way not already covered by our [Privacy Policy](/info/gdpr).
